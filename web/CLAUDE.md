# Frontend (web/)

Data contract: `docs/contracts.md`. Test data in `web/public/data/` is generated by `scripts/mock-data.py` (documented in `scripts/MOCK-DATA.md`). Do not edit it manually or make code depend on specific values or point counts.

## Rules
- Map: OpenLayers. Register the raster CRS in proj4 using `meta.raster_proj4`. Coordinates (x_m, y_m) are already in meters in this CRS; do NOT recalculate them from lat/lon. Draw points with a custom canvas layer and use a custom index for hit testing.
- Read the `candidates.bin` column order exclusively from `meta.candidates.columns`; never hardcode column indices. `id` ≠ row index (use the `idToIndex` map in the store).
- Read binary data through `DataView` with explicit little-endian byte order.
- TS strict + `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, no `any`. Pure functions (colors, nearest point, parsing) have Vitest tests.

## Code map
- `src/data/` – contract types and loader (`load.ts`: `loadMeta`, `loadCandidates`, `loadSites`, `getProjString`).
- `src/store.ts` – application state (zustand).
- `src/map/` – `createMap.ts` (OL, CRS, 84°S circle, layers, hover/click), `PointsLayer.ts` (canvas, red-to-green color buckets; filtered-out points are not drawn), `projection.ts` (lat/lon → CRS meters on a sphere), `sitesLayer.ts`, `colors.ts`, `nearestPoint.ts`.
- Globe (`src/views/Home.tsx`): the south pole (80–90°S) has a separate LROC WAC mosaic cap from `public/assets/south_pole_2k.jpg` (stereographic-projection UVs, edge faded from 84°S). `scripts/prepare-south-pole-texture.py` generates it from `south_pole_moon.jpg` by removing grid lines, applying gamma 0.55, and resizing to 2048 px.
- `src/views/SouthPole.tsx` – a scrollable pole view: the first screen contains the map with overlays (`app/SearchBox`, `app/TopCard`, `app/Legend`) and the `app/AdvancedDrawer` drawer; below the map, `panel/Places` contains glass-style location cards. Styles are in `views/south-pole.css`, tokens in `index.css`.
- Locations A/B: `store.selectedIndex` (A), `compareIndex` (B), `picking` (waiting for B after “Compare place”). Points selected through the map/ranking/search go through `pickPoint`/`focusIndex`, which select A or B. Coordinates instead of IDs: `app/coords.ts`.
- `src/panel/` – `PlaceBox` (location card; comparison mode has no slider or timeline because `Places` shows one shared bar below both cards in normal page flow: `TimeBar` + `PlaceTimeline` for A and B), `SkyPanorama` + `skyDraw.ts` (canvas), `sky.ts` (pure sky geometry: Sun, Earth with phase and size, stars; tests), `analysis.ts` (masks and series for A and B, shared time cursor, separate view directions in `views.a`/`views.b`), `stars.ts` (`public/assets/stars.json` catalog, Hipparcos down to magnitude 6).
- The panorama is viewed from the selected point: the observer remains there while only the viewing direction rotates. The bottom-left compass (`panel/SkyMiniMap.tsx`) is deliberately minimal: outline, view cone, N mark, and Sun/Earth dots, without textures or labels; clicking rotates the view. Sun and Earth tracks (`SkyTrack` in `skyDraw.ts`) use the series for the next 27 days and break wherever the body is behind terrain. There are deliberately no Sun/Earth buttons.
- Panorama: star orientation is approximate (rotation around the Z axis aligned with the ephemeris Sun, error around 1–2°, because the lunar axis ≈ ecliptic pole). No Milky Way or terrain texture (silhouette from the 720-bin mask).
- Initial pole view: terrain only. Ranking colors (red = poor to green = best, `map/colors.ts`) and Top 10 appear only after “Show ranking” (`store.rankingShown`, animation in `PointsLayer.setRevealed`); the X in the Top 10 card hides them. Canvases (panorama, `useCanvas`) render at most once per frame using the latest state and do not cancel a scheduled frame after each change, because that starved rendering during playback. Named locations are hidden by default (map toggle, `layers.sites`).
- Ranking modes (`store.colorBy`, the `app/ModeSwitch` control at the bottom of the map): combined score or one metric. Each has its own color scale and accent (`map/themes.ts`: combined red→green, Sun yellow, Earth blue, night purple, communication teal, slope orange, PSR ice blue) and its own Top 10 (`recompute` ranks by `norms[colorBy]`, where 1 is best, so #1 is the smallest value for lower-is-better metrics). Short mode names describe the optimum (“Short night”, “Flat”); full names use `modeFull.*` keys.
- Years (Delivery 6, contracts 1.4 and 3.4): `ephem.bin` covers 10 years (`n_steps` = 87,672), while `candidates.bin` and `validation.json` cover only `candidates_period` (2028, steps 0..8783). Compare files over `candidatesRange(meta)` from `data/years.ts`, not the entire ephemeris. The store loads `metrics_by_year.bin` into `byYear`; `store.year` replaces four annual metrics in `candidates` through `candidatesForYear`, while `baseCandidates` remains unchanged for the map and validation. Annual metric color ranges use all years. `app/YearPicker` appears in the ranking-mode bar and beside the time slider. Analysis series cover all 10 years, while the timeline, slider, and playback use the selected year's `activeRange`; `yearSpans` counts a night or communication gap crossing a year boundary in full. Permanent shadow (`isEternalShadow`) means `sun_pct = 0` or a night equal to the year's length.
- Panel power (Delivery 7, contract 3.4): `power_track_wm2` and `power_fixed_wm2` are full metrics (Power and Fixed panel ranking modes, minimum filters, default weight 0 to avoid counting sunlight twice); `power_fixed_az_deg` is auxiliary. They exist only in `metrics_by_year` (`YEAR_ONLY_COLUMNS` are NaN in `candidates` until a year replaces them); without these columns, `store.metricKeys` hides them. `physics/solarPower.ts` reproduces the engine calculation from the series (5° bins, centers at 5b+2.5, denominator = steps in the year, NaN azimuth at zero power) for older data, card diagnostics, and validation to hundredths of W/m². Horizontal panels are deliberately omitted.
- Map (`createMap.ts`): panning is disabled when fully zoomed out (`constrainOnlyCenter: false` + disabled DragPan), and scrolling the wheel down scrolls the page. Selecting a point smoothly scrolls to the cards below the map.
- Candidate points (`map/PointsLayer.ts`): level 0 (2 km grid) as a smooth field (`map/grid.ts`), level 1 (250 m refinement) as small squares on top; Top 10 as numbered badges.
- UI text uses `t()`/`fill()` from `src/i18n.ts` and is English only (the Polish version and language switch were removed on November 15, 2026). “Advanced” enables ranking automatically, because weights and filters have no visible effect without it.
- Entries in sites.json with “[MOCK]” in their names are hidden (`app/sites.ts`).
